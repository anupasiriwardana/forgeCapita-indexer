## 1. Executive Summary

The `forgecapita-indexer` monitors factory launches and secondary market trades across Arbitrum Sepolia and BSC Testnet. It dynamically attaches event listeners to cloned child contracts (`MilestoneEscrow`, `DividendDistributor`, `ProjectToken`, `RevenueRouter`) and writes structured data directly to the shared Neon PostgreSQL database.

The core indexing pipeline, multi-chain configuration, start-block pinning, staking accounting, and P2P orderbook tracking are **operational**. `fundingGoal` is now read from the escrow at launch (no longer `0n`), and votes plus the v2 lifecycle (completed, failed, cancelled, shutdown) are handled (WS-02.3, unrun until the first sync). `RevenueRouter` payments and claims are now indexed with per-project and per-month aggregates (WS-02.5, unrun until the first sync). **P2P trade history (IDX-06), the locked-down public endpoints, the first real sync and tests, and BSC public RPC rate-limiting still require attention.**
